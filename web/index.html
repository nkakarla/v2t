Windows app

Prefer to convert on your own PC?

The Windows app does the same job without uploading anything. It works offline, handles many videos at once, and can combine them into one PDF.

Download for Windows

V2T.exe · Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) · free

Windows will warn you about the app. That's expected.

V2T isn't code-signed yet (signing certificates cost money, and this is a free tool), so Windows and your browser don't recognize it. The warnings below are the normal ones for any new, unsigned app. It only takes a few clicks, and you only have to do it once.

  1. Keep the download. Your browser may say “V2T.exe isn't commonly downloaded”.
    • Edge: hover the download, click … → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway.
    • Chrome: in the downloads list click Keep (or Download suspicious file).
  2. Move it somewhere local, like your Desktop or Documents folder. Avoid running it from Google Drive, OneDrive or a network drive, because Windows adds an extra security prompt there that's easy to miss.
  3. Double-click V2T.exe. A blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears. Click More info, then Run anyway.
    Windows protected your PC Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting…
    More info → App: V2T.exe · Publisher: Unknown publisher
    Run anyway
  4. Wait for it to open. A loading screen appears right away. The first start can take up to 30 seconds while the app unpacks. Don't double-click again; it will open on its own.

Privacy

  • On this page: your video is converted on the EPerf Labs server. The video is deleted as soon as it's converted, and the PDF and text file within an hour. Nothing is shared or used for anything else.
  • Windows app: runs entirely on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Text recognition is Tesseract OCR, not an AI service. No AI credits are used.

Tips & known limits

  • Best with articles in light mode on a phone. Scroll smoothly; pausing isn't needed.
  • Text recognition reads English. Tables and charts come through as screenshots in the PDF, not as text.
  • OCR isn't perfect. Expect the odd typo, like “Al” for “AI”.
  • Recordings up to 15 minutes and 300 MB. A 30-second recording takes about half a minute to convert.
  • If your antivirus quarantines the Windows app, that's a false alarm common to unsigned apps. Restore it or allow it.
